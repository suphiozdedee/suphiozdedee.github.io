Privacy & Data Policy

Your Personal Data Is Protected

This document explains how data of PlanicWorks users and companies is collected, processed and protected.

Last Updated: August 26, 2026

1. Data Controller

Data controller under Turkish privacy law: Projenic Tasarım Mimarlık Ticaret Limited Şirketi; Kadıköy, İstanbul, Türkiye; privacy@planicworks.com; https://planicworks.com.

2. Personal Data Collected

  • Account and identity: Name, email, company details and optional profile photo.
  • Billing: Legal company and tax details, billing email and address. Card or bank details are not stored by PlanicWorks.
  • Operations: Projects, briefs, CRM, quotes, files, activities and notifications.
  • Technical and security: IP, device, session and anonymized error logs.

3. Purposes of Processing

  • Account creation, authentication and secure sign-in.
  • Workspace, subscription, project, brief, quote and operations management.
  • Billing information and service notifications.
  • Service quality, security and legal compliance.

4. Legal Grounds

  • Performance of a contract.
  • Legitimate interests in security and service improvement.
  • Explicit consent only for marketing where required.
  • Legal duties for tax and accounting.

5. Sharing with Third Parties

  • Data is not shared with ad networks or data brokers.
  • Supabase provides database, authentication and PlanicWorks Cloud infrastructure.
  • Google Drive is used only when selected and authorized by the customer.

6. Storage Options and Access

  • With customer Drive, content remains in the authorized area and PlanicWorks performs only requested actions within granted scopes.
  • Super Admin cannot view customer files in the normal panel. Customers can revoke Drive access.
  • Google account security, MFA, recovery, sharing, quota and customer-initiated deletion remain the customer’s responsibility.
  • With PlanicWorks Cloud, content is stored in managed cloud infrastructure; limited infrastructure access is possible only for operations, security, backup, support or legal needs.
  • File metadata may be retained for security, quota and workspace functions.
  • Customer content is not used for advertising, profiling or sale.

7. International Transfers

Supabase and Google Drive may process data abroad. Transfers rely on an applicable mechanism under KVKK Art. 9, with required records maintained under current law; continued use alone is not explicit consent.

8. Retention

  • Active account data: During subscription and applicable legal periods.
  • Billing and accounting: For periods required by law.
  • Security logs: Only as long as required for security and law.
  • Deletion requests: Within the target period except for legal retention duties.

9. Your Privacy Rights

  • Learn whether and why data is processed and request information.
  • Learn recipients.
  • Request correction, deletion or destruction.
  • Object to automated outcomes and unlawful processing.
  • Seek compensation for damage.
  • Requests may be sent to privacy@planicworks.com and will be answered within 30 days.

10. Data Security

  • TLS, secure session management and encryption at rest where supported.
  • Rate limiting and security logging.
  • Multi-tenant architecture, RLS and company isolation.
  • Super Admin cannot access customer briefs, CRM, projects, quotes, messages or file contents through the application.

11. Children’s Privacy

PlanicWorks is a B2B business application, is not directed to anyone under 18 and does not knowingly collect data from children.

12. Policy Changes

Material changes are notified by registered email and the updated date is published here.

13. Contact and Requests

For questions, requests or complaints, contact privacy@planicworks.com or visit https://planicworks.com. Your right to complain to the Turkish Data Protection Authority remains available.
